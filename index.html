About RGIPT

Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology, Jais

RGIPT is an autonomous institution established by the Government of India and accorded the status of an Institution of National Importance, on lines similar to the IITs, under the Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology Act, 2007.

The Institute's mission is to build world-class human resources for India's petroleum and energy sector through B.Tech, IDD, M.Tech, MBA and Ph.D. programs across engineering, sciences, humanities and management.

RGIPT also runs two extension centers — the Assam Energy Institute in Sivasagar and an Energy Institute in Bengaluru — extending its academic and research reach across the country.

About Urja Shakti

Energy needs more women leading it.

Urja Shakti is RGIPT's national initiative to celebrate and grow women's presence in the energy sector. Launched in 2021, it gives young women a platform to explore the sector's technologies, markets and policies — and the 2026 edition marks its fourth year.

Participants develop and pitch original ideas across six energy themes, building the kind of confident, forward-looking thinking the sector needs — and contributing to a wider push for gender equality across energy careers.
